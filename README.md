# ITIL 4 Foundation Prep

A self-contained study app for the ITIL 4 Foundation exam: a study plan, notes, flashcards and a practice exam in the formats used in the real exam.

**Open the app:** https://hugomonre.github.io/ITIL-4-Foundation-Prep/

It works on desktop and mobile, with no sign-in. On a phone, open the link and add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

## What's inside

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | A dated week-by-week study plan, exam facts and exam habits |
| **Notes** | The full syllabus: key concepts, the four dimensions, the service value system and value chain, the 7 guiding principles, the purpose of 15 practices and the 7 practices in detail |
| **Flashcards** | 41 cards, filterable by topic. Mark the ones you know and the rest keep coming back. |
| **Practice exam** | 40 questions drawn from a bank of 90, weighted like the real exam, with options shuffled each time |

### Practice exam modes

- **Practice mode:** shows the answer and an explanation after each question
- **Exam mode:** 60-minute timer, results only at the end, like the real exam
- **Retry my mistakes:** asks only the questions you got wrong until you get them right

Questions use the real exam formats: situations, "which two statements are correct", missing words, and wrong answers that are the definition of a nearby term.

## The exam

| | |
|---|---|
| Questions | 40, multiple choice |
| Time | 60 minutes, closed book |
| Pass mark | 26 out of 40 (65%) |
| Ready when | 32+ out of 40 in Exam mode, three times in a row |

## Use it offline

The whole app is a single file, `index.html`. Download it from this repository and open it in any browser. It works without an internet connection.

## Notes

- Progress (flashcards, mistakes list, best score) is saved in your browser only. It does not sync between devices, and clearing browser data resets it.
- These are unofficial study notes and practice questions, written for revision. They are not official exam material. Use the official sample papers from your training provider as well.
